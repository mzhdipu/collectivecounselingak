Meet Tia Kraus, LCSW

Compassionate, person-centered counseling rooted in Fairbanks, Alaska, and available by telehealth across the state.

Feeling overwhelmed, or just need someone to talk to?

Schedule a session with Tia and let’s work through life’s challenges together, side by side.

Tia Kraus, LCSW, was born and raised in Fairbanks, Alaska, and has Indigenous roots and connection to Nenana, Alaska. She enjoys traveling, hiking, laughing, going to concerts, and hanging out with her cats.

She uses a collaborative therapeutic approach that is person-centered, strength-based, and promotes autonomy that honors the individual. Her eclectic approach is crafted to help each person get what they need in a supportive and understanding environment. Tia aims to empower her clients and give them the confidence and tools they need to succeed.

A collaborative, person-first approach

We believe in “person first” therapy that centers around the client and their needs. Here is what that looks like in practice.

Person-centered

Therapy centers around you and your needs. You help set the direction, and we move at a pace that feels right.

Strength-based

We build on what is already working in your life and the resilience you already carry with you.

Honors your autonomy

Your voice, choices, and values lead the way. The goal is confidence and tools you can keep using long after sessions end.

Eclectic and tailored

No one-size-fits-all model. Each plan is crafted to fit the individual in a supportive, understanding environment.

Our story

The story behind the name

Collective Effervescence is a sociological concept coined by Émile Durkheim. It describes the intense, electric, and transformative energy experienced when a group of people gathers for a shared, often emotional, purpose. It fosters feelings of unity, belonging, and shared joy, which are essential for human connection.

“When I first learned this term, I could instantly remember feeling this way while surrounded by people where I felt like I belonged for the first time. Humans have such an innate desire to belong with one another. I wanted to create a space where clients feel heard, seen, and connected. Thus, the name Collective Effervescence Counseling came to life.”
