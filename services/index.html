Services

Counseling Services

In-person sessions in Fairbanks and telehealth statewide for children ages 10+, teens, adults, and families.

Session types

Whether you are reaching out for yourself, your child, or your whole family, every session is grounded in warmth, collaboration, and respect for where you are.

60 to 90 minutes

Mental Health Assessment

A thorough first conversation to understand your history, what brings you in, and your goals. It lays the foundation for a plan that fits you.

53 minutes

Individual Sessions

One-on-one counseling for pre-teens, teens, and adults. Sessions are available in person in Fairbanks or by secure telehealth.

53 minutes

Family Sessions

Held with or without the primary client. A supportive space to strengthen communication, understand one another, and heal together.

Two ways to meet

Choose what works best for your schedule and comfort. You can also switch between the two.

In person in Fairbanks

Meet face to face in a calm, private setting in Fairbanks.

  • Individual and family sessions
  • Evening and Sunday appointments

Telehealth across Alaska

Connect from home by secure video, wherever you are in the state.

  • No travel or weather worries
  • Same care and continuity as in person

Specialties

Support for the experiences that weigh on children, teens, adults, and families, with special care for the communities below.

  • Anxiety
  • Depression
  • Processing childhood trauma and difficult events
  • PTSD
  • Self-esteem
  • Attachment style
  • Processing breakups
  • Divorce and major life events
  • Life transitions
  • Regaining your sparkle

Populations served

  • Alaska Native & Indigenous clients
  • Pre-teens & teens (ages 10+)
  • Women’s issues
  • LGBTQ+
  • Adoption
  • Foster care
  • Autoimmune disorders
  • Chronic illness

Insurance & payment

Counseling should be accessible. Collective Effervescence Counseling is in network with several major plans, and offers a self-pay option.

Not sure what your plan covers? Reach out and we will help you figure it out during your free consultation.

When we can meet

Appointments are designed around school and work. There is a short waitlist for new clients.

  • Sunday to Thursday, 12 pm to 8 pm
  • Evening and after-work slots at 5, 6 & 7 pm
  • Sunday and weekend appointments available
  • Free 15-minute consultation for new clients
