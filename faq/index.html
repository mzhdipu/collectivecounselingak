FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers about services, insurance, scheduling, and how to get started.

What services do you offer?

We offer a range of services designed to meet your needs, including mental health assessments (60 to 90 minutes), individual sessions (53 minutes), and family sessions with or without the primary client (53 minutes). Sessions are available in person in Fairbanks or by telehealth anywhere in Alaska.

How do I get started?

Getting started is simple. Reach out through the contact form or schedule a free 15-minute consultation.

  • Call or text: (907) 921-1298
  • Email: tiakraus@collectivecounselingak.com

We’ll walk you through the next steps and answer any questions along the way.

What insurance do you accept?

Collective Effervescence Counseling is in network with:

  • Medicaid
  • Aetna
  • Cigna
  • Moda
  • United Healthcare / Optum
  • Blue Cross Blue Shield

Superbills are also available if you would like to seek reimbursement from an out-of-network plan.

How much does a session cost without insurance?

Self-pay (cash) sessions are $150. If you have out-of-network benefits, we can provide a superbill you can submit to your insurance company.

What ages do you work with?

Tia works with children ages 10 and up, pre-teens, teens, adults, and families.

What do you specialize in?

  • Anxiety
  • Depression
  • Processing childhood trauma and difficult events
  • Self-esteem
  • Attachment style
  • Processing breakups, major life events, and divorce
  • Regaining your sparkle

Populations served include women’s issues, LGBTQ+, adoption, foster care, autoimmune disorders, chronic illness, and Indigenous clients.

What are your hours?

Sunday to Thursday, 12 pm to 8 pm.

  • Evening and after-work therapy slots available at 5 pm, 6 pm, and 7 pm
  • Sunday and weekend therapy appointments available

Do you offer telehealth?

Yes. In-person sessions are offered in Fairbanks, and telehealth sessions are available statewide across Alaska. Tia is licensed in both Alaska and Colorado.

Is there a waitlist?

There is currently a short waitlist for new clients. Reach out for a free 15-minute consultation and we will let you know the next available times.

Still have questions?

Reach out any time. A free 15-minute consultation is the easiest way to see if we are a good fit.
