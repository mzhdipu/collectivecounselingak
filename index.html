Meet your counselor

Hi, I’m Tia Kraus, LCSW

I’m a Licensed Clinical Social Worker in both Alaska and Colorado. My practice is based in Fairbanks, offering in-person counseling sessions with the option of telehealth statewide.

My approach to counseling is warm, collaborative, and very “human first.” I meet each client where they are, and we work through life’s challenges together, side by side.

  • Born and raised in Fairbanks, with Indigenous roots in Nenana
  • Working with children 10+, teens, adults, and families

Why “Collective Effervescence”?

Collective effervescence is a concept coined by sociologist Émile Durkheim. It describes the warm, electric energy people feel when they come together with a shared purpose: a sense of unity, belonging, and joy that is essential to human connection.

Humans have an innate desire to belong. This practice was created to be a space where clients feel heard, seen, and connected.
